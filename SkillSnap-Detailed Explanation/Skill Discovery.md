# 🔍 Skill Discovery — SkillSnap

## 📌 Overview

**Skill Discovery** is a feature that helps users find new subjects, hobbies, activities, and skills that they may be interested in learning.

Many people want to learn something new but do not know **what to choose or where to start**.

SkillSnap makes this process easier by allowing users to explore different learning categories in one place.

---

## 🎯 Why Skill Discovery?

A user may already know that they want to learn, but may not know which skill suits their interests.

For example:

```text id="h1q8dj"
Interested in Art
       ↓
Drawing
Painting
Digital Art
Photography
Animation
Graphic Design
```

Instead of limiting the user to one option, SkillSnap allows them to explore related skills.

---

## 🧭 How Skill Discovery Works

The basic flow can be:

```text id="t0h3kq"

   ↓

   ↓

   ↓

   ↓

   ↓
Start Learning
```
