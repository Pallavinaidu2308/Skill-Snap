# 🔮 Future Scope — SkillSnap

## 📌 Overview

SkillSnap can be expanded beyond its initial learning platform into a complete ecosystem for **education, hobbies, creativity, personal development, and skill discovery**.

The future scope focuses on making learning more personalized, interactive, social, and accessible.

---

AI can analyze a user's interests, learning history, progress, and goals to recommend relevant skills.

Example:

```text id="j7k3np"

Graphic Design
```

### Benefit

Users can discover relevant skills without having to search manually.

## ✨ Future Vision

SkillSnap can evolve from simply helping users **learn skills** to helping them:

> **Discover → Learn → Practice → Improve → Showcase → Connect → Grow**

The goal is to create an environment where users can continuously develop themselves across academics, creativity, hobbies, practical skills, sports, and personal interests.

> **SkillSnap's future is a world where learning is not limited to one subject, one platform, or one stage of life.**
