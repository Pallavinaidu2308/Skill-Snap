# 👥 Target Users — SkillSnap

## 📌 Overview

SkillSnap is designed for **anyone who wants to learn, improve, or explore a skill**.

The platform is not restricted to a particular age group, educational background, or type of learning. Users can choose skills based on their own interests, goals, and available time.

---

## 🎓 1. School & College Students

Students can use SkillSnap to combine their academic learning with personal interests.

### Example

```text
📚 Academics
├── Mathematics
└── Programming

🎨 Personal Skills
├── Drawing
└── Guitar
```

### How It Helps

* Supports academic learning
* Encourages hobbies
* Helps discover new interests
* Develops skills outside the classroom
* Allows flexible learning alongside studies

---

## 💻 2. Working Professionals

Professionals can use SkillSnap to develop skills outside their regular work.

For example:

```text
Professional Skill
      +
Photography
      +
Fitness
      +
Cooking
```

### How It Helps

* Learn new skills after work
* Explore hobbies
* Develop personal interests
* Maintain continuous learning
* Balance professional and personal development

---

## 🌱 3. Beginners

People who have never tried a particular skill can use SkillSnap to start from the basics.

For example:

```text
Never Played Guitar
        ↓
Beginner Lessons
        ↓
Basic Practice
        ↓
Regular Practice
        ↓
Improvement
```

### How It Helps

Beginners can explore skills without needing prior knowledge.

---

## 🎨 4. Hobby Learners

Some users may not be learning for academics or careers. They may simply want to enjoy a hobby.

Examples:

* Drawing

* Dance
* Photography
* Cooking
* Gardening
* Sports

### How It Helps

SkillSnap gives hobby learners a structured place to explore and improve their interests.

---

## 🏆 5. Skill Enthusiasts

Some users may already have experience in a particular skill and want to improve further.

For example:

For example:

```text

  ↓
```

# 🚀 Core Idea

SkillSnap is built for people who believe that **learning can happen anywhere, at any stage, and in many different forms**.

Whether someone wants to pass an exam, learn programming, play guitar, improve drawing, learn cooking, practice a sport, or simply discover something new, SkillSnap can provide a place to begin.

> **SkillSnap is for anyone who wants to learn something, improve something, or discover something new.**
